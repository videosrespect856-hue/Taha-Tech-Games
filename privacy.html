Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 3, 2026

Welcome to Daily Notes Pro, published by GOFIT SPORTSWEAR LTD. We respect your privacy and are committed to providing a simple, safe, and reliable note-taking experience. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles information when you use Daily Notes Pro and our website.

Information We Collect

Daily Notes Pro does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information from users. The app is designed to work without requiring users to create an account, sign in, or provide personal details.

We do not collect:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Home or mailing addresses
  • Location information
  • Contacts
  • Photos, videos, or media files
  • Payment information
  • Login credentials
  • User-generated content stored outside the user's device

Local Storage

Daily Notes Pro stores notes locally on the user's device to provide core note-taking functionality. Your notes remain on your device and are not uploaded, transmitted, or shared with GOFIT SPORTSWEAR LTD.

Device Information

GOFIT SPORTSWEAR LTD does not intentionally collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, or hardware information. Any basic technical processing handled by the Android operating system or Google Play is controlled by those platforms and is not collected or stored by GOFIT SPORTSWEAR LTD.

Advertising

Daily Notes Pro does not display advertisements. We do not use advertising networks, personalized ads, advertising SDKs, or advertising identifiers.

In-App Purchases

Daily Notes Pro does not include in-app purchases, subscriptions, premium upgrades, or paid digital content. GOFIT SPORTSWEAR LTD does not collect or process payment information.

Analytics and Tracking

Daily Notes Pro does not use analytics services, tracking tools, user behavior monitoring, marketing trackers, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not track how users interact with the app.

Third-Party Services

Daily Notes Pro does not integrate third-party services for advertising, analytics, marketing, profiling, or user tracking.

Children's Privacy

We care about children's privacy. Daily Notes Pro does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 or from users of any age.

Since our app does not collect personal data, we do not store or share children's personal information.

Data Sharing

GOFIT SPORTSWEAR LTD does not sell, rent, trade, transfer, or share user information with third parties. Since we do not collect personal information, there is no personal user data available to disclose.

Data Security

We aim to provide a secure user experience. Because Daily Notes Pro does not collect or store personal information on external servers, there is no personal user database maintained by GOFIT SPORTSWEAR LTD.

International Users

Daily Notes Pro may be available to users in different countries. Regardless of where you use the app, GOFIT SPORTSWEAR LTD does not collect, store, or share personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
