Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 3, 2026

Welcome to TAHA TECH GAMES. We respect your privacy and are committed to providing simple, safe, and enjoyable gaming experiences. This Privacy Policy explains how our games handle information when you use our apps, games, and website.

Information We Collect

Our games do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information from users. Our simple idle games are designed to work without requiring users to create an account, sign in, or provide personal details.

We do not collect:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Home or mailing addresses
  • Location information
  • Contacts
  • Photos, videos, or media files
  • Payment information
  • Login credentials
  • User-generated content

Device Information

TAHA TECH GAMES does not intentionally collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, or hardware information. Any basic technical processing handled by the Android operating system or Google Play is controlled by those platforms and is not collected or stored by TAHA TECH GAMES.

Advertising

Our games do not display advertisements. We do not use advertising networks, personalized ads, advertising SDKs, or advertising identifiers.

In-App Purchases

Our games do not include in-app purchases, subscriptions, premium upgrades, or paid digital content. TAHA TECH GAMES does not collect or process payment information.

Analytics and Tracking

Our games do not use analytics services, tracking tools, user behavior monitoring, marketing trackers, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not track how users play or interact with our games.

Third-Party Services

Our games do not integrate third-party services for advertising, analytics, marketing, profiling, or user tracking. We also do not promote third-party games inside our games.

Children's Privacy

We care about children's privacy. Our games do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 or from users of any age. Since our games do not collect personal data, we do not store or share children's personal information.

Data Sharing

TAHA TECH GAMES does not sell, rent, trade, transfer, or share user information with third parties. Since we do not collect personal information, there is no personal user data available to disclose.

Data Security

We aim to provide a secure gaming experience. Because our games do not collect or store personal information, there is no personal user database maintained by TAHA TECH GAMES.

International Users

Our games may be available to users in different countries. Regardless of where you use our games, TAHA TECH GAMES does not collect, store, or share personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our games, legal requirements, or operational updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
